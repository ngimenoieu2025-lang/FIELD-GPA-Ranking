\subsection*{Teamwork and Review Process}

We organised the work through the project repository (\url{https://github.com/ngimenoieu2025-lang/FIELD-GPA-Ranking}). After the Progress Report, we opened one GitHub issue for each new analysis and assigned it to one member: the US replication to Adam Bajer, the mixed-effects model and the normal-approximation check to Pablo Torres, and the comparison of ranking rules to Ignacio Gimeno. Each analysis was developed on its own branch and merged through a pull request; another member reviewed the code, reran it and checked that the saved results reproduced, and in one case asked for changes before approving it. After the feedback on the Sentence Outline, we added the adjusted-mark analysis and the tuned comparison of prediction models, and we rebalanced the writing so that each of us was responsible for a similar share of the report. All three of us read the complete report and approved it before submission.
